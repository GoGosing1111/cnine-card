Create ONE irresistibly cute tiny penguin-suited mascot with the face/hair characteristics of Image 3 lovingly translated into the big-eyed stylized doll/chibi language of Images 1 and 2. This is a plush mascot illustration, not a photographic head pasted onto a costume. Preserve the recognizable eye shape, fringe or parting, hair color, eyebrows, mouth and overall facial impression, with natural illustrated integration.

COSTUME AND PROPORTIONS ARE LOCKED to Images 1 and 2: very short round egg-shaped body, oversized round hood/head, almost no visible neck, tiny flipper arms, tiny yellow webbed penguin feet, large soft white oval belly patch, black/deep navy plush one-piece penguin suit and matching hood. Hood has two white oval penguin eyes with black pupils and one small yellow beak on top of the forehead. Keep the light silver-gray collar, little red inset and silver front ring/carabinier detail, neatly drawn and clearly legible. The whole character is about 1.8 heads tall, round and squat; do not turn it into a slender girl or normal human proportions. Do not add human hands, fingers, long legs, shoes or a different outfit. Preserve the chubby silhouette even with the subject's own hairstyle, tuck hair into the suit except short face-framing locks.

STYLE: polished soft painterly 2.5D game illustration with a beautiful dimensional plush surface, clean silhouette, smooth cheek shading, warm blush and lively glossy eyes. Cute premium character art, lovingly painted rather than a physical toy photographed in a room; no photo realism. A consistent soft light from upper left, subtle material highlights; clear enough to read as a small pet sprite.

OUTPUT: one character only, full body including every flipper, hood and toe, centered with 10% comfortable transparent margin on all sides, square 1024x1024 composition. Genuine RGBA transparency outside the character, clean alpha edges, no backdrop, no scene, no floor, no opaque white fill, no baked checkerboard, no cast shadow, no letters, no names, no UI, no labels, no logos, no watermark. No adult body or revealing clothing from the face reference.

Give her a bright open-mouth smiling expression. Both yellow feet grounded and visible. The flower must not hide the face. Keep the body round and plump.
