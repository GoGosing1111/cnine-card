# 롯데 아윤 장비창 리소스

내장 `image_gen` 사용. 입력은 사용자가 첨부한 승인 로비 원본 한 장이며, 생성 출력의 실제 알파를 그대로 보존했다. 원본은 덮어쓰지 않는다.

```text
Use case: background-extraction / identity-preserve.
Asset type: full-body transparent equipment-window avatar for a Korean RPG.
Input image 1 is the approved LOTTE AYOON avatar and the sole identity/costume source.
Primary request: Create one polished full-body standing equipment sprite of this SAME ADULT WOMAN, faithfully preserving her youthful adult face, facial features, warm smile, long dark brown wavy hair, tall slender proportions, cream short-sleeved collared blouse with burgundy collar/cuffs, embroidered seagull crest, fitted burgundy mini skirt with gold trim and buttons, pendant necklace, bracelet, and white ankle-strap high heels. Preserve the source illustration rendering and beautiful face, not a different person.
Composition: vertical 1024x1536 canvas. Single centered character, entire head, all hair, both hands, both shoes fully inside canvas with clear space around every edge. Natural elegant standing pose with one hand softly near the hair and the other relaxed, legs slightly less crossed for clear equipment-window silhouette. No camera angle distortion. Maintain long slender legs and source proportions. Soft clean studio lighting on the character.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha PNG, empty pixels outside character including between arm/torso and between legs, NO room, floor, cast shadow, glow, halo, white/black backplate or checkerboard drawn into pixels. Keep only character, hair, clothing and worn jewelry.
Constraints: Do not change face, hair, palette, uniform design, skirt into pleats, or shoes. No text, labels, UI, card frame, watermark, extra objects or additional figures. Actual native transparency is essential.
```
