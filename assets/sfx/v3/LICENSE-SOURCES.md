# V3 RPG combat SFX source record

The V3 role combat audio sprite uses original mixes built from the following
CC0/public-domain field recordings. The final timing, layering, filtering,
sub-impact design, room tails and mastering are produced for cnine-card.

- Swishes Sound Pack by artisticdude — CC0
  - https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack
- Fantasy Sound Effects (Tinysized SFX) by Vehicle — CC0
  - https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-sound-effects-tinysized-sfx
- Metal Impact Sounds by BMacZero — CC0
  - https://opengameart.org/content/metal-impact-sounds
